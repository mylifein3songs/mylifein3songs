Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

1. Who We Are

My Life in 3 Songs (“we”, “our”, “us”) operates this website. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

2. Information We Collect

When you register, we collect your full name, email address, date of birth, country, and optionally your city. We also collect the songs you select and any special instructions you provide. We do not collect payment information. When you visit the website, we also automatically collect limited technical information through Google Analytics — including your approximate location (city or region level), device type, browser, the pages you view, how you arrived at the site, and how long you stay. This information is used in aggregate to understand how the site is being used and is described further in Section 7.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide the My Life in 3 Songs service — specifically to store your funeral song preferences and allow authorised lookups by people who know your email address and date of birth. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Sharing & Lookups

Your song choices, name, date of birth, and location are visible to anyone who enters your registered email address and date of birth on the lookup page. This is a deliberate feature of the service. By registering, you consent to this lookup functionality. You should only register an email address that you are comfortable sharing with trusted individuals.

5. Data Storage & International Transfers

Your data is stored on secure servers. As a global service, your data may be stored and processed in countries other than your own, including New Zealand, where the operator is based. Where data is transferred internationally, we take appropriate steps to ensure it remains protected in accordance with this policy. By using the service, you consent to your data being stored and processed in the countries where our service infrastructure operates.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You can update your details via the “Edit My Details” section when logged in, or permanently delete your account from the same section. To request data deletion by email, contact us via the Support page. Depending on your country, you may have additional rights under applicable data protection law — including the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR, or equivalent legislation in your jurisdiction. We will honour all valid requests in accordance with applicable law.

7. Analytics & Cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics sets cookies on your device that allow it to recognise returning visitors and track which pages are viewed during a session. The data collected includes your approximate geographic location (derived from your IP address but not stored at full resolution), device and browser information, referring website, and pages visited. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising purposes, and we have not enabled Google Signals or cross-device tracking. Data collected through Google Analytics is retained for 14 months. For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by using your browser’s “Do Not Track” settings or built-in tracking protections.

8. Third-Party Services

We embed music previews from Apple Music. When you interact with an Apple Music player, Apple’s own privacy policy applies. We do not control what data Apple may collect through their embedded player. Please review Apple’s Privacy Policy for details.

9. Children

My Life in 3 Songs is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has registered, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of material changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service following changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Support page.
